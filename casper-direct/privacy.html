Casper Direct – Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

Casper Direct is a personal desktop app. It has no servers and collects no data about anyone.

What it accesses

When its owner connects a YouTube or TikTok account, the app receives a sign-in token and the account's display name. It uses them only to upload the videos the owner chooses and to show which account is connected.

Where data is stored

Tokens and settings are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to the developer or any third party other than YouTube and TikTok themselves.

Removing access

Pressing Disconnect in the app deletes the stored token. Access can also be revoked at any time in Google account settings or TikTok's Security & Permissions settings.
